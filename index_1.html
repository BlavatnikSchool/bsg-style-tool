Upload a policy paper, academic paper, or report and receive a structured style review against Oxford University house style. Issues are flagged by category and priority – no changes are made to your document.

This tool is in beta. No AI check can capture 100% of issues – always use it alongside a human proofread. Results are a guide only and should always be checked against the full style guides: Oxford University Style Guide and the Blavatnik School Written Style Guide 2025 (Oxford login required). Please do not upload documents containing sensitive or personal information – while your files are not stored anywhere, passing such content to an external API is not best practice.

Accepts .docx or .txt up to 10 MB. Documents up to approximately 6,000 words are checked in full. Longer documents will be partially checked – the word count is shown on upload.
